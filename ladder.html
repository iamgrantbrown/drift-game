#

Rungs

Climb from the bottom word to the top. Each rung joins the one below and the one above into a word or phrase.


      
    


    

      one a day · a new ladder at midnight pacific

      prototype, working title · the original Drift · source
    

  


  
    
    
How to play

    

      

        1
        
Six words, bottom to top
Each one joins the next into a word or phrase: coffee cup, cupcake, cakewalk.

      

      

        2
        
You get the ends
The bottom word and the top word are written in. The four rungs between them are missing.

      

      

        3
        
A rung locks when it joins both neighbours
Start at the bottom, or anywhere. A miss tells you which side it did join, so every try teaches you something.

      

    

    
No limit on tries. Fewer is better. Hints open on a rung after a couple of misses, only if you ask.
