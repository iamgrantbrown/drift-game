    How to play
    
    
      
      
    1. You start on the tee word. The flag is on the hole word.
      2. 
      
    2. A stroke is a word. It has to make a word or phrase with the one you’re standing on, reading forward: from school you could play bus (school bus), then stop (bus stop), then watch (stopwatch).
      3. 
      
    3. You’re in when your word joins the hole word. Any route counts. The yardage tells you how many strokes you are from the hole, so you can see whether a shot took you closer.
      4. 
    
    
    
    Par is the setter’s route. Beat it and that’s a birdie. A word the course doesn’t know costs nothing. Play into a dead end and you can take a drop back a word for one stroke.
