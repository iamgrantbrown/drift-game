How to play

    
Every night the word drifts. Yesterday’s word and
    today’s word always join — into one word or a common phrase.
    race drifts to track (racetrack), track drifts to
    suit (tracksuit). You get six guesses to catch today’s word.

    
Every guess tells you how close its meaning is —
    ice · cold · cool · warm · hot · scorching.
